# Highlights & Montages

Select highlights for their action, emotion, contrast, or consequence—not just visual brightness. Inspect the full source range and build a timestamped selects list before assembling the edit. Separate lead-in, action, and reaction; preserve enough setup to understand a payoff.

Choose an arc appropriate to the footage: escalation to a best moment, thematic grouping, or a purposeful musical montage. Open with a strong moment or credible tease. Remove dead travel and repeated attempts unless they explain progress. Avoid implying a continuous successful run when the footage contains separate attempts; disclose the montage when continuity could mislead.

Cut on actions and changes of direction; let musical accents reinforce the selection. Use speed ramps only where motion remains intelligible. Check retimed footage for stutter and audio artifacts. Preserve reactions or landings when they complete an action. Do not automatically make every shot short.

Reframe around the actual moving subject across the shot. Preserve essential HUD information or crop it completely; avoid fragments. Use restrained punch-ins, occasional tracking or graphic accents, and sparse text. A wide composition does not automatically survive a vertical crop.

Retain useful source sound and use transitions, impacts, or silence around a payoff. End on a satisfying action, reaction, musical resolution, or deliberate replay loop—not an arbitrary file boundary.

Handoff: final video, editable timeline, source-range selections, and brief decisions. Review action continuity, subject visibility, crop edges, repeated frames, and the exact ending in playback.

Use [Blender Video](../blender-vse-video/SKILL.md) for version-aware execution, [Audio & Sound Design](../blender-vse-audio-sound-design/SKILL.md) when mixing, and [Video Review & Delivery](../blender-vse-video-review-delivery/SKILL.md) before handoff. Preserve the brief’s duration, style, format and source constraints; these are editorial guidance, not a fixed template. Follow source inspection → plan → scripted timeline → preview → inspection → revision → final render.
