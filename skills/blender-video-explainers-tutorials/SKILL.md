# Explainers & Tutorials

Define what the viewer should understand or be able to do afterward, their starting knowledge, and the necessary prerequisites. Use the supplied source material as authority. Identify missing steps instead of inventing a procedure.

Organize into a clear sequence: outcome, prerequisites if necessary, actions or concepts, verification, and next step. Preserve the ordering needed to reproduce a workflow. A short conceptual explainer may instead use a concrete example and causal progression. Avoid forcing a tutorial into trailer pacing.

Match each visual to the current explanation. Reveal diagrams progressively and keep labels attached to their referents. Use Blender animation/compositing when movement explains a relationship; decorative 3D is optional. Give viewers time to read a command, setting, or formula. Do not hide meaningful operations with a cut.

For hands-on steps, show the initial state, action and resulting state. Mark skipped repetitive work or elapsed time. Keep pointer focus, zoom and captions coordinated. Avoid simultaneous competing text and narration. Use source-grounded captions and distinguish exact commands from paraphrase.

Use an example or completed result to confirm understanding at the end. Review for omitted prerequisites, inaccurate labels, too-fast holds and contradictory narration. If a procedure has not actually been performed, do not label it tested.

Handoff: final video, editable timeline, step outline and source/dependency notes. Include chapters or transcript only when requested or genuinely useful to the delivery format.

Use [Blender Video](../blender-video-tools/SKILL.md) for version-aware execution, [Audio & Sound Design](../blender-video-audio-sound-design/SKILL.md) when mixing, and [Video Review & Delivery](../blender-video-video-review-delivery/SKILL.md) before handoff. Preserve the brief’s duration, style, format and source constraints; these are editorial guidance, not a fixed template. Follow source inspection → plan → scripted timeline → preview → inspection → revision → final render.
